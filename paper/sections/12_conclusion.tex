\section{Conclusion \& Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper makes two contributions that reinforce each other. The evaluation half
(Sections~\ref{sec:evalprotocol}--\ref{sec:topological}) is a class-agnostic,
tolerance/topology-aware metric library that found and fixed two real failure modes through an
explicit synthetic-sweep $\to$ real-data validation $\to$ targeted additive fix $\to$
regression-test loop. The training half (Section~\ref{sec:method}) asks whether the same geometric
properties can be trained \emph{into} a model directly, rather than only measured afterward.
\texttt{PLEMMultiTaskLoss} combines three differentiable analogs of those metrics (tolerance,
topology, and --- via a genuine reformulation rather than a relaxation --- point-instance
localization) into one multi-task loss, with a masked multi-source training scheme letting datasets
that individually annotate only some of the three feature types jointly supervise one model.
Section~\ref{subsec:lossablation-results}'s synthetic ablations confirm each term encodes the
geometric sensitivity it was designed to. Section~\ref{subsec:joint-results}'s small real run shows
all four terms learning jointly and turns up a real-data example of why CBHM's harsh composite and
DTAF1's lenient one are both needed. Section~\ref{subsec:scaled-results} carries the mechanism to a
1,033-tile, three-source production pipeline: its final model reaches SpaceNet
\texttt{cbhm\_soft} 0.685 and detects road intersections --- the point feature, derived from the
same vector labels as the roads --- at F1 0.279. Its six-run development record is as much a result
as the final numbers: a point channel inside the segmentation softmax floods it, a point label that
exists on one source only and spans tens of pixels is unlearnable, the heatmap loss's negative
normalization has no safe default at full resolution, and a widely used public dataset leaves a
third of its road-tile area without building labels. Each was caught by measuring rather than
assuming --- per-term loss logs, per-source predicted-pixel counts, point scores restricted to tiles
that actually contain points, and qualitative panels --- and most of those checks are built on
Sections~\ref{sec:evalprotocol}--\ref{sec:topological}'s protocol.
Section~\ref{subsec:baseline-results} trains the same pipeline the conventional way: on SpaceNet the
two models tie on mIoU, while \texttt{cbhm\_soft}, \texttt{dtaf1\_topo}, clDice, BF, APLS, and
intersection F1 all favour the joint loss, and on SpaceNet6 a model that fails by IoU either way
scores 0.350 against 0.071 on boundary F1. The metrics this paper proposes therefore separate
models that pixel overlap rates as equal; whether the joint loss's margin exceeds training-seed
variance is the next thing to measure.

The highest-priority next steps:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Raise road-intersection recall.} A third of junctions get no peak at any threshold
    (Section~\ref{subsec:scaled-results}). Candidates: an intermediate negative-term weight between
    the per-pixel mean and CenterNet's normalizer, or a lower-resolution heatmap head closer to
    CenterNet's own design.
  \item \textbf{Repeat the baseline comparison over more training seeds}
    (Section~\ref{subsec:baseline-results}). With one seed per model, mIoU ties while every PLEM
    metric favours the joint loss on SpaceNet; two or three more seeds per model would show whether
    that gap exceeds training variance.
\end{itemize}

Further future work:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Re-run \texttt{dtaf1\_topo} on the real 24-tile SpaceNet perturbation sample used for
    Section~\ref{subsec:realdata-results}'s other real-data findings.
  \item Fix SpaceNet6's road-on-buildings confusion (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}), e.g.\ a
    SAR-specific encoder branch or explicit ``no road'' supervision on SAR tiles.
  \item Separate the effect of the building-label repair from model improvement, e.g.\ by
    re-scoring run 5's checkpoint against the repaired ground truth.
  \item Explore learned/adaptive tolerance radii instead of fixed per-class constants
    (Section~\ref{subsec:tolerance-radii}), and correspondingly a learned rather than fixed
    \texttt{iters} for \texttt{SoftClDiceLoss} (Section~\ref{subsec:cldice-loss}).
  \item Characterize DTAF1-Topo's inherited APLS failure modes on short/sparse real road segments
    with a dedicated sensitivity sweep, mirroring \texttt{sweep\_sparse\_class\_offset}'s treatment
    of CBHM's analogous weakness (Section~\ref{subsec:class-imbalance}).
  \item Tune \texttt{PLEMMultiTaskLoss}'s per-term \texttt{weights} now that the point channel gives
    a real signal to optimize against.
  \item Investigate whether \texttt{SoftClDiceLoss} alone provides meaningful indirect connectivity
    pressure during training, as a cheaper partial substitute for the connectivity term
    \texttt{losses/} otherwise cannot have
    (Sections~\ref{subsec:apls},~\ref{subsec:method-principle}).
  \item Resample the three sources to a common GSD, or ablate whether the current mismatch matters.
\end{itemize}
