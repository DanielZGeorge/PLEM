\section{Conclusion \& Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper has two halves that support each other. The evaluation half
(Sections~\ref{sec:evalprotocol}--\ref{sec:topological}) is a metric library that tolerates small
positional errors, checks road connectivity and scores every feature class with the same code. It
found and fixed two real failure modes through an explicit loop of
synthetic sweeps $\to$ real-data validation $\to$ targeted additive fix $\to$ regression test. The
training half (Section~\ref{sec:method}) asks whether a model can be trained to have the same
geometric properties directly, instead of only being measured on them afterward.
\texttt{PLEMMultiTaskLoss} combines three differentiable analogs of those metrics, for tolerance,
topology and point-instance localization (the last a reformulation rather than a relaxation), into
one multi-task loss, and a masked multi-source training scheme lets datasets that each annotate
only some of the three feature types jointly supervise one model. The synthetic ablations of
Section~\ref{subsec:lossablation-results} confirm that each term encodes the geometric sensitivity
it was designed for. The small real run of Section~\ref{subsec:joint-results} shows all four terms
learning together and gives a real-data example of why both CBHM's harsh composite and DTAF1's
lenient one are needed. Section~\ref{subsec:scaled-results} carries the mechanism to a 1,033-tile,
three-source production pipeline, whose final model reaches SpaceNet \texttt{cbhm\_soft} 0.685 and
detects road intersections, the point feature derived from the same vector labels as the roads, at
F1 0.279. Its six-run development record is a result in its own right. A point channel inside the
segmentation softmax floods it; a point label that exists on one source only and spans tens of
pixels cannot be learned; the heatmap loss's negative normalization has no safe default at full
resolution; and a widely used public dataset leaves a third of its road-tile area without building
labels. We caught each of these by measurement, using per-term loss logs, per-source counts of
predicted pixels, point scores restricted to tiles that contain points, and qualitative panels, and
most of those checks are built on the protocol of
Sections~\ref{sec:evalprotocol}--\ref{sec:topological}. Section~\ref{subsec:baseline-results}
trains the same pipeline the conventional way. On SpaceNet the two models tie on mIoU, while
\texttt{cbhm\_soft}, \texttt{dtaf1\_topo}, clDice, BF, APLS and intersection F1 all favour the
joint loss, and on SpaceNet6, where both models fail by IoU, boundary F1 is 0.350 against 0.071.
The metrics this paper proposes therefore separate models that pixel overlap rates as equal.

The highest-priority next steps are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Raise road-intersection recall.} A third of junctions get no peak at any threshold
    (Section~\ref{subsec:scaled-results}). Candidate fixes are an intermediate negative-term weight
    between the per-pixel mean and CenterNet's normalizer, or a lower-resolution heatmap head closer
    to CenterNet's own design.
  \item \textbf{Repeat the baseline comparison over more training seeds}
    (Section~\ref{subsec:baseline-results}). Two or three more seeds per model would show whether
    the SpaceNet gap on PLEM's metrics exceeds training variance.
\end{itemize}

Further future work:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Re-run \texttt{dtaf1\_topo} on the real 24-tile SpaceNet perturbation sample used for the
    other real-data findings of Section~\ref{subsec:realdata-results}.
  \item Fix SpaceNet6's confusion of buildings with roads (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}), for
    example with a SAR-specific encoder branch or explicit ``no road'' supervision on SAR tiles.
  \item Separate the effect of the building-label repair from model improvement, for example by
    re-scoring run 5's checkpoint against the repaired ground truth.
  \item Explore learned or adaptive tolerance radii in place of fixed per-class constants
    (Section~\ref{subsec:tolerance-radii}), and with them a learned rather than fixed
    \texttt{iters} for \texttt{SoftClDiceLoss} (Section~\ref{subsec:cldice-loss}).
  \item Characterize the APLS failure modes that DTAF1-Topo inherits on short or sparse real road
    segments with a dedicated sensitivity sweep, as \texttt{sweep\_sparse\_class\_offset} does for
    CBHM's similar weakness (Section~\ref{subsec:class-imbalance}).
  \item Tune the per-term \texttt{weights} of \texttt{PLEMMultiTaskLoss}, now that the point
    channel gives a real signal to optimize against.
  \item Investigate whether \texttt{SoftClDiceLoss} alone gives useful indirect pressure toward
    connectivity during training, as a cheaper partial substitute for the connectivity term that
    \texttt{losses/} otherwise cannot have
    (Sections~\ref{subsec:apls},~\ref{subsec:method-principle}).
  \item Resample the three sources to a common GSD, or test whether the current mismatch matters.
\end{itemize}
